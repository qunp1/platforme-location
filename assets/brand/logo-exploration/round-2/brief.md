# Round two — response to user feedback

The first round was rejected. A felt like a generic N. B had promise but its
internal route was unintelligible. C was too abstract. Preserve earlier files.

Revised brief: welcoming and readable, with a doorway as a promising starting
point. Remove the interior route entirely. Compare genuinely different treatments,
including a name-led logo rather than requiring every idea to use a separate icon.

Exploration: plain round entrance (too generic); pointed open gateway (build A);
door leaf inside frame (risks repeating the rejected interior object); doorway
as lowercase n within the name (build B); paired openings (looks like m); threshold
underline (unnecessary decoration); keyhole (rental cliché); house roof (property
association); warm serif wordmark with a returning y (build C); coastal wave
(too abstract). Build only the three strongest candidates.

A: a mildly pointed entrance with a completely empty interior. One filled outline
on a 256 grid, even jamb widths, no key/path/object. Risk: heritage/hospitality.
B: lowercase name, a broad arched n, and a y that returns underneath the last pair
of letters. Custom filled paths. Risk: return gesture loses significance small.
C: a warmer editorial name-led alternative; outlined Georgia Bold letter shapes
with custom spacing. Risk: less obviously a digital
mobility brand; symbol development would follow selection.

Greyscale concepts first. No final palette, full kit or direction is approved.
