# Nadorly — arrival gate variants

Concepts, not an approved final identity. The user chose round-three B as the
starting direction and requested close variants before deciding.

B1 preserves the chosen drawing. B2 softens the apex, enlarges the upper opening
and gives the car horizontal headlights. B3 merges the arch and windscreen,
removing the triangular opening. B4 separates the vehicle and frame.

Recommendation: B2 for a more car-like front; B3 for simplicity. B3 risks
reading as a bus or train; B4's gaps and headlights need a simplified 16px cut.
All retain the risk of architectural or transport-service associations.

Wordmark is the earlier exploratory outlined lettering, held constant to compare
symbols. Green is a provisional colour preview, not a selected palette.
No new trademark clearance performed in this iteration.
